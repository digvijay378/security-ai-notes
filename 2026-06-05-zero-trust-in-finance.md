# 2026-06-05 — Zero Trust realities in large financial institutions

Theory: "Never trust, always verify."

Reality in big banks:
- Thousands of service accounts with broad entitlements
- "Break glass" procedures that bypass everything
- Decades of accumulated exceptions for "business critical" applications

The wins come from:
- Starting with high-value transaction paths instead of boiling the ocean
- Using data (logs + flow data) to prove what actually talks to what
- Automating the validation so you can re-check after every change

Without automation, Zero Trust becomes a paperwork exercise.
